    08 / QA Findings
    Website and Digital QAThese issues were found during research. Blockers must be fixed before any ad spend begins.
    🔴
    BLOCKER: No Conversion Tracking on the Website
    No Google Tag Manager, GA4 events, or call tracking found on 10xhealthyskin.com. Google Ads cannot learn or optimize without conversion data. Every rupee spent before this is fixed is wasted. Fix: Install GTM, configure "Book Appointment" form submit and phone click events, link to Google Ads as primary conversions. Must be done before spending anything.
    🔴
    BLOCKER: One Page, No Service-Specific Landing Pages
    The website is a single scrolling page. Every ad, regardless of which service someone searched for, points to the same homepage. A person searching "acne treatment coimbatore" lands on a general page with no acne-specific content above the fold. This lowers Quality Score, raises CPC, and increases bounce rate. Fix: Build at least 4 service pages or anchor sections with a relevant headline, price range, and a "Book Now" form visible without scrolling.
    🔴
    BLOCKER: Spelling Errors Found on Website Content
    Content QA found spelling mistakes that reduce credibility with new visitors from paid ads. Examples: "acne scare treatment" instead of "acne scar treatment" and similar copy errors in treatment descriptions. Visitors from Google Ads are evaluating the clinic in seconds. Spelling errors on a medical website reduce trust immediately. Fix: Full copy review and correction before campaigns go live.
    🟡
    WARNING: No Pricing on the Website
    60% of CBE competitors also hide pricing. Being the transparent clinic is a clear advantage. Even a price range like "Laser Hair Removal from Rs.800 per session" reduces bounce from tire-kicker visitors and attracts patients who are ready to book.
    🟡
    WARNING: Google Business Profile Completeness Unknown
    Top competitors like Keva Clinic (5.0 stars, 332 reviews) and Dr. Thaj (4.6 stars, 485 reviews) have rich, complete GBP listings driving organic Maps traffic. A complete GBP is required for Maps Ads to perform well. Fix: Audit and complete all 48 GBP fields, add 20 or more clinic photos, enable booking, activate Q and A.
    ✅
    READY: Phone CTA and "Book Appointment" Button Are Live
    +(91) 95141 43576 and "Book Appointment" visible on all page sections. Call Extensions and Location Extensions can be configured in Google Ads from Day 1 using existing site content.

    15 / Safety Net
    Recovery PlanThree response levels set before launch. Fast action regardless of who notices the problem first.
    Level 1: WatchCPL rises 20 to 35% above target for 1 week
    • Check Search Terms report. Block irrelevant queries like "hair removal machine price".
    • Check ad copy CTR. Rotate the weakest RSA headline variant.
    • Confirm landing page loads in under 3 seconds on mobile.
    • Confirm call conversion tag (60 sec) is firing correctly.
    • Budget: no change. Normal weekly CPL variance.
    Level 2: ActCPL above the max guardrail for 2 weeks straight
    • Pause the lowest-performing service ad group.
    • Move its budget to the best-performing service campaign.
    • Tighten radius targeting to core CBE city only.
    • Switch to Maximize Conversions if still on Manual CPC.
    • Verify conversions are recording in Google Ads and GA4.
    Level 3: ResetCPL above guardrail for a full calendar month
    • Full account audit: keywords, negatives, ad copy, landing pages, extensions.
    • Rebuild campaigns with tighter match types and new RSA copy.
    • Call the last 20 bookings and ask how they found the clinic.
    • Pause Call Ads for 2 weeks to test Search-only and isolate the issue.
    • Written findings report before resuming full budget.
    Day-one safeguards are set up at launch: Daily budget cap. Target CPA ceiling from Unit Economics. Automated CPL alert when 7-day average exceeds the guardrail. Location exclusions outside the defined radius.
