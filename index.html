The Opportunity

Organic and handloom cotton clothing is a growing, underserved niche in Indian e-commerce. Consumers are actively searching for terms like "handloom crop top online," "organic cotton kurti," "ajrakh printed shirt" — with far less paid competition than mass-market fashion, which means lower CPCs and a faster path to profitable ROAS.

Google Ads offers three structural advantages for Vastara right now:

  • Intent-led traffic: Shoppers on Google are further along the purchase journey than on social media — they are searching with intent to buy.
  • Product feed-driven discovery: Performance Max surfaces Vastara's products across Search, Shopping, YouTube, Gmail, Display, and Maps in a single campaign — ideal for a brand with 100+ SKUs.
  • Measurable returns from day one: Unlike brand awareness spends, every rupee on Google is traceable to an actual Shopify purchase.

03

Proposed Campaign Strategy

The strategy runs in two layers: a capture layer (Search + PMax) for in-market buyers actively searching, and an awareness layer (Demand Gen / YouTube) to build the top of funnel and re-engage site visitors.

Campaign 1 — Brand Search Always On

Protect the brand name. When someone searches "Vastara" or "Vastara The Label," this campaign ensures the brand appears at the top — not a competitor or aggregator. Cost is minimal; ROAS is very high (15–40× is typical for brand search in this niche).

Campaign 2 — Performance Max Core Revenue Driver

PMax is Google's AI-driven campaign type that uses the Shopify product feed to automatically show the right product to the right person across all Google surfaces. The majority of budget goes here. PMax requires a 4–6 week learning period; conversions accelerate meaningfully once the algorithm has sufficient data.

Campaign 3 — Non-Brand Search Month 3+

Targeting high-intent keywords like "handloom crop top online India," "organic cotton women's shirt," "ajrakh printed top buy." Introduced once PMax has a stable conversion base to avoid cannibalising its learning signal.

Campaign 4 — Demand Gen / YouTube Month 3–4

Remarketing to site visitors and lookalike audiences using product and brand creative on YouTube and Google's discovery surfaces. Supports brand recall and re-engages window shoppers who didn't convert on first visit.

Recommended launch sequence: Brand Search + PMax in Month 1 → Non-Brand Search in Month 3 → Demand Gen in Month 3–4. Launching all campaigns simultaneously dilutes budget and interrupts the PMax learning phase.

Why This Approach Works

  • Shopify-native integration: The store connects directly to Google Merchant Center — product images, prices, and inventory sync automatically with no manual feed management after initial setup.
  • PMax is built for this catalogue: 100+ SKUs, strong product visuals, clear price differentiation — all the signals PMax needs to perform well. Brands with larger catalogues consistently outperform smaller ones in PMax.
  • Sale pricing is a paid ad asset: The brand runs 63–82% discounts. In ad copy, "From Rs. 699 | Organic Cotton" is a powerful hook — higher CTR means lower effective CPC, which compounds over time.
  • Brand search ROI is disproportionate: With 1,100+ reviews and growing organic presence, brand search volume already exists and is currently unmonetised. Capturing it is the fastest, cheapest win.
  • Niche = lower CPCs: "Handloom organic cotton top" has far fewer advertisers than "women's top online" — a meaningful CPC advantage despite pan-India targeting.
  • Festive season timing: Launching now gives campaigns 4–6 weeks of learning before Navratri and Diwali — typically 2–3× normal e-commerce traffic in India.

Performance Recovery Plan

Campaigns can dip due to algorithm changes, feed issues, creative fatigue, seasonality, or competitor activity. This plan defines the response at each severity level so action is fast and consistent — regardless of who notices the drop first.

Level 1 — Watch Trigger: ROAS drops 15–25% vs prior 2-week average, for 1 week
  • Check Merchant Center for new feed disapprovals or price mismatches
  • Review search terms report — block irrelevant or low-intent queries
  • Check if Brand Search impression share has dropped (competitor activity)
  • Review PMax asset performance — pause assets rated "Low"
  • Budget: no change. One week of dip is within normal algorithmic fluctuation.
Level 2 — Act Trigger: ROAS below break-even for 2 consecutive weeks
  • Reduce Demand Gen budget by 50% immediately — highest risk, lowest direct ROAS
  • Shift freed budget to Brand Search — most reliable, highest ROAS campaign
  • Reduce PMax budget by 25–30% and hold for 7 days to allow re-optimisation
  • Verify purchase conversion tag is still firing correctly (GA4 real-time + Tag Assistant)
  • Check if top-selling products are out of stock, removed from feed, or repriced
  • Review site: sale expired, checkout broken on mobile, new delivery issues
Level 3 — Reset Trigger: ROAS below break-even for a full calendar month
  • Full account audit: feed quality, keyword coverage, audience signal quality, creative fatigue
  • Reset PMax with fresh asset groups, tighter audience signals, updated product exclusions
  • Run a Standard Shopping campaign in parallel as a controlled test against PMax
  • Pause non-brand Search entirely until PMax stabilises
  • Revisit unit economics — has AOV dropped? Have COGS or shipping costs risen?
  • Present a written findings report and revised 60-day plan before resuming full budget
Structural safeguards set on day one — Monthly budget cap in Google Ads so spend never exceeds the approved amount · Target ROAS floor on PMax bid strategy to prevent buying low-quality traffic · Automated Google Ads alert if 7-day CPO exceeds the agreed guardrail threshold.
