06 / QA Findings
Pre-Launch ChecklistIssues found during deep research QA — must resolve before go-live
🔴
BLOCKER: Inventory Availability — Nearly 0 products in-stock
Live Shopify API audit confirms 7,458+ products all showing available: false — only 1 exception (N1065, Emerald Gold Necklace, ₹880). More critically, a daily automated batch job is running at ~20:00 IST and re-zeroing all inventory — confirmed on two consecutive days (4 Aug & 5 Aug 2026). This is a recurring system process, not a one-off reset. Google Merchant Center will disapprove all products; Shopping & PMax cannot serve. Three resolution paths:
(1) Switch products to inventory_policy: continue (sell when out of stock);
(2) Fix the inventory sync so actual stock quantities are written back after each batch run;
(3) Confirm make-to-order model and set Merchant Center feed availability: preorder.
🔴
BLOCKER: Website Requires Login to Browse
All pages (homepage, product pages, collections) redirect to shop.app authentication. Google's Merchant Center crawler cannot verify product pages without public access. Verify Shopify's Google channel uses server-side feed (bypasses this) OR remove storefront password before campaign launch.
🟡
WARNING: Bulk inventory update happened today (5 Aug 2026)
A daily automated batch job is confirmed zeroing all inventory — 4 Aug at 20:50 IST and 5 Aug at 19:57 IST (two consecutive days, not a one-off). Recovery PlanThree severity levels defined before launch — fast response regardless of who notices first
Level 1 — WatchROAS drops 15–25% vs prior 2-week avg · sustained 1 week

  • Block low-intent search terms from Search Terms report
  • Check Brand impression share
  • Pause PMax asset groups rated "Low"
  • Budget: no change. Normal weekly fluctuation.
Level 2 — ActROAS below break-even · 2 consecutive weeks
  • Cut Demand Gen / Display budget 50% immediately
  • Shift freed budget to Brand Search
  • Reduce PMax 25–30% · hold 7 days before evaluating
  • Verify purchase conversion tag still firing (GA4 + Google Ads)
  • Check top 10 products: stock status or price changes?
Level 3 — ResetROAS below break-even · full calendar month
  • Full account audit: feed · keywords · audiences · creatives
  • Reset PMax with fresh asset groups and signals
  • Run Standard Shopping as parallel test campaign
  • Pause non-brand Search entirely
  • Written findings report before resuming full budget
