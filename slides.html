03
03 — Overview

Campaign Strategy

Capture layer (Search + PMax) targets buyers actively searching. Awareness layer (Demand Gen) builds top-of-funnel and retargets.

Brand Search Always On

Protect brand name from competitors and aggregators. Minimal spend, 15–40× ROAS typical. First campaign to launch.

Performance Max Core Driver

AI campaign using Shopify feed to serve ads across all Google surfaces. Majority of budget here. Needs 4–6 week learning phase.

Non-Brand Search Month 3+

"Handloom crop top online", "organic cotton kurti" — launch after PMax has sufficient conversion data.

Demand Gen / YouTube Month 3–4

Retarget site visitors and lookalike audiences. Supports brand recall, re-engages window shoppers.

Sequence: Brand Search + PMax (Month 1) → Non-Brand Search + Demand Gen (Month 3). Launching all at once dilutes budget and stalls PMax learning.

08
08 — Execution

Why This Works

  • Shopify-native feed — product images, prices, inventory sync automatically to Merchant Center. No manual maintenance.
  • 100+ SKUs = PMax fuel — PMax performs best on large catalogues with clear visual differentiation. Vastara is built for it.
  • Sale pricing is ad copy — "From ₹699 | Organic Cotton" is a high-CTR hook. Lower CPC compounds over time.
  • Unmonetised brand demand — 1,100+ reviews means brand search volume exists today, currently unprotected.
  • Niche CPCs — "handloom organic cotton top" has far fewer bidders than "women's top online" — even pan-India.
  • Festive timing — go-live now gives 4–6 weeks to optimise before Navratri and Diwali (2–3× normal e-commerce traffic).

Recovery Plan

Three severity levels. Defined in advance so response is fast regardless of who notices first.

Level 1 — WatchROAS drops 15–25% vs prior 2-week avg · 1 week
  • Check feed for disapprovals or price errors
  • Block low-intent search terms
  • Check Brand impression share
  • Pause PMax assets rated "Low"
  • Budget: no change. Normal fluctuation.
Level 2 — ActROAS below break-even · 2 consecutive weeks
  • Cut Demand Gen budget 50% immediately
  • Shift freed budget to Brand Search
  • Reduce PMax 25–30% · hold 7 days
  • Verify purchase tag still firing
  • Check top products: stock / price changes?
Level 3 — ResetROAS below break-even · full calendar month
  • Full account audit: feed · keywords · audiences · creatives
  • Reset PMax with fresh asset groups
  • Run Standard Shopping as parallel test
  • Pause non-brand Search entirely
  • Written findings report before resuming full budget
Day-one safeguards: Monthly budget cap · Target ROAS floor on PMax · Automated alert if 7-day CPO exceeds guardrail.
