    05 / Strategy
    5-Channel Campaign ArchitectureService businesses need a fundamentally different approach than ecommerce — appointments, not checkouts
    Search Ads
    High-Intent Appointment Seekers
    Keyword-targeted ads for “[service] + coimbatore” queries. Separate ad groups per service (Hair Removal, Acne, Hydrafacial, Anti-aging). RSAs with clinic USPs + location. Call extension on every ad. Dayparting: clinic hours only (9am–7pm).
    Primary channel from Day 1
    Call Ads
    Direct Mobile Bookings
    Mobile-only ads that dial the clinic instantly — no website required. Conversion = phone call ≥60 seconds. Captures patients who search on mobile and prefer to call. Typically 30–40% lower CPL than form-fill campaigns for local clinics.
    Primary channel from Day 1
    Local & Maps Ads
    “Near Me” Search Domination
    Google Maps placement for “skin clinic near me”, “laser near me”, “dermatologist near me” queries. Location extension amplifies organic map presence with paid prominence. Critical for walk-in traffic from nearby Race Course Rd, RS Puram & Gandhipuram corridors.
    Phase 1 — alongside Search
    Performance Max (Local)
    Automated Appointment Conversions
    PMax optimised for local appointments — runs across Search, Maps, YouTube and Display simultaneously. Feed Instagram before/after images as creative assets. Only viable after 30+ tracked conversions (Smart Bidding needs data). Not Day 1.
    Phase 2 — Month 2–3
    Demand Generation
    Awareness + Remarketing across YouTube, Instagram & Discover
    Remarketing to website visitors. Lookalikes from @10x_healthyskin Instagram followers. Before/after video ads. Seasonal pushes for Navarathri, bridal season, summer skin care. Runs Phase 2 onwards from budget freed by maturing Search efficiency.

    08 / QA Findings
    Pre-Launch ChecklistFindings from website & digital presence QA — blockers must ship before any ad spend begins
    🔴
    BLOCKER: No Conversion Tracking
    No Google Tag Manager, GA4 events, or phone call tracking detected on 10xhealthyskin.com. Google Ads cannot optimise toward bookings without conversion data — campaigns will burn spend on clicks with no intelligence. Fix: Install GTM → configure “Book Appointment” form submit + phone click events in GA4 → import to Google Ads as primary conversion actions. This must be done before spending ₹1 on ads.
    🔴
    BLOCKER: Single-Page Site — No Service-Specific Landing Pages
    All ads (Laser Hair Removal, Acne, Hydrafacial, Men’s Grooming) must point to the same homepage. This mismatches ad intent, lowers Google Quality Score, raises CPC, and increases bounce. Fix: Build 4 dedicated pages (minimum) or anchor sections with service-specific headline, price range, before/after image, and above-the-fold “Book Now” form. Alternatively, even scroll-targeted landing pages (e.g. /#laser-hair-removal) with relevant H1s will improve Quality Score significantly.
    🟡
    WARNING: No Pricing Visible
    60% of CBE competitors also hide pricing — which means being the transparent clinic is a differentiator, not a risk. Paid traffic with price anchoring (e.g. “Laser Hair Removal from ₹800 per session”) converts at a higher rate and attracts better-qualified leads than “call us for pricing”.
    🟡
    WARNING: Google Business Profile Not Fully Optimised
    GBP is listed but completeness is unknown. Top competitors (Keva: 332 reviews 5.0★; Dr. Thaj: 485 reviews) have rich profiles driving organic Maps traffic. Without a complete GBP, Local Ads + Maps Ads underperform. Fix: Audit and complete all 48 GBP fields, add 20+ clinic photos, enable booking, activate Q&A section.

    • Budget: no change. Normal CPL weekly variance.
    Level 2 — ActCPL above max guardrail · 2 consecutive weeks

    • Shift paused budget to best-performing service campaign
    • Tighten radius targeting to core CBE city only (exclude Tirupur, Erode)
    • Switch to Maximize Conversions bidding if on Manual CPC
    • Verify conversions are recording correctly in Google Ads & GA4
    Level 3 — ResetCPL above guardrail · full calendar month
    • Full account audit: keywords, negatives, ad copy, landing pages, extensions
    • Rebuild campaigns with tighter phrase-match keywords and new RSAs
    • Survey last 20 bookings — which channel / search term brought them?
    • Pause Call Ads for 2 weeks — test Search-only to isolate problem
    • Written findings report & revised strategy before resuming full budget
