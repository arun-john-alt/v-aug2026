03 — Overview

Campaign Strategy

Two layers: capture (Search + PMax) for buyers actively searching, and awareness (Demand Gen) for the top of funnel.

Brand Search Always On

Protect brand name. Minimal cost, 15–40× ROAS. First thing to set up.

Performance Max Core Driver

AI-driven — uses Shopify feed to show the right product across all Google surfaces. Majority of budget goes here. 4–6 week learning phase.

Non-Brand Search Month 3+

"Handloom crop top online India", "organic cotton kurti" — once PMax has a stable conversion base.

Demand Gen / YouTube Month 3–4

Remarket to site visitors and lookalike audiences. Supports brand recall, re-engages window shoppers.

Launch sequence: Brand Search + PMax (Month 1) → Non-Brand Search + Demand Gen (Month 3). Don't launch everything at once.

08 — Execution

Why This Works

  • Shopify-native integration — product feed syncs automatically to Google Merchant Center. No manual maintenance after setup.
  • PMax is built for this catalogue — 100+ SKUs, strong visuals, clear price differentiation. Larger catalogues outperform consistently.
  • Sale pricing is a hook — "From ₹699 | Organic Cotton" in ad copy drives higher CTR, lowering effective CPC over time.
  • Brand search is unmonetised today — 1,100+ reviews means brand search volume exists. Capturing it is the cheapest, highest-ROI first win.
  • Niche = lower CPCs — "handloom organic cotton top" has far fewer advertisers than "women's top online."
  • Festive season timing — launching now gives 4–6 weeks of learning before Navratri and Diwali (2–3× normal traffic).

Recovery Plan

Three severity levels. Defined in advance so response is fast regardless of who notices first.

Level 1 — WatchROAS drops 15–25% for 1 week
  • Check feed for disapprovals / price mismatches
  • Review search terms — block low-intent queries
  • Check Brand impression share (competitor activity)
  • Pause PMax assets rated "Low"
  • Budget: no change
Level 2 — ActROAS below break-even, 2 weeks
  • Cut Demand Gen budget 50% immediately
  • Shift to Brand Search — highest ROAS
  • Reduce PMax 25–30%, hold 7 days
  • Verify purchase tag still firing
  • Check top products — out of stock / repriced?
Level 3 — ResetROAS below break-even, 1 month
  • Full account audit: feed, keywords, audiences, creatives
  • Reset PMax with fresh asset groups
  • Run Standard Shopping as parallel test
  • Pause non-brand Search entirely
  • Written findings report before resuming full budget
Day-one safeguards: Monthly budget cap · Target ROAS floor on PMax · Automated alert if 7-day CPO exceeds guardrail.
