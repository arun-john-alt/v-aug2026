04 / Strategy
Campaign Architecture
1
Search
High-Intent Appointment Seekers
Target "[service] + coimbatore" keywords with exact and phrase match. Separate ad groups per service: Laser Hair Removal, Acne, Hydrafacial, Pigmentation. RSAs with location + USP copy: "Board-Certified Dermocosmetologist · Race Course Rd · Book Free Consultation". Call extension on every ad.
2
Call Ads
Direct Appointment Booking for Mobile
Mobile-only call-only ads targeting high-intent queries. Conversion = phone call ≥60 seconds. Eliminates website bounce — caller goes directly to clinic. Run during clinic hours (10am–7pm). Lower CPL than form submissions for local services.
3
Remarketing + Display
Website Visitors & Lookalike Audiences
Retarget visitors who didn't book. Upload @10x_healthyskin Instagram followers as Customer Match seed for lookalikes. Use before/after creative with service-specific CTAs. Demand Gen campaign for awareness reach across YouTube + Discover. Budget from Phase 2 only.
No Google Shopping or Performance Max — not applicable for service businesses. Remarketing starts Phase 2 once Search builds sufficient audience.

06 / QA Findings
Pre-Launch ChecklistIssues found during website QA — must resolve before campaign launch
🔴
BLOCKER: No Conversion Tracking in Place
No Google Tag Manager, GA4 events, or call-tracking detected on the website. Without conversion tracking, Google Ads cannot optimise toward appointments — campaigns will run blind on clicks alone. Resolution: Install GTM, configure GA4 form submission event + phone click event. Add Google Ads conversion tag for “Book Appointment” button and phone call ≥60 sec. Do this before spending a single rupee.
🔴
BLOCKER: Single-Page Website — No Service-Specific Landing Pages
10xhealthyskin.com is a one-page scrolling site. All ads must point to the same URL regardless of which service was searched. A user searching “acne treatment coimbatore” lands on the homepage and must scroll to find acne info — poor match, lower Quality Score, higher CPC. Resolution: Create dedicated landing pages per top service (Laser Hair Removal, Acne, Hydrafacial) or at minimum anchor links (#laser, #acne, #hydrafacial) with above-the-fold service content and a “Book Now” form.
🟡
WARNING: No Pricing on Website
Paid traffic users expect price anchoring before deciding to book. No pricing = higher bounce from curious low-intent visitors who won't convert, driving up CPL. Consider adding “Free Consultation + from ₹500” messaging or a treatment price guide, even as a range. Reduces tire-kicker leads.
✅
READY: Phone Number & “Book Appointment” CTA Visible
+(91) 95141 43576 prominently placed across all sections. “Book Appointment” button present. Google Call Extensions and Location Extensions can be configured immediately using existing site assets.
✅
READY: Proprietary Treatments Are Strong Ad Copy Differentiators
10x Glow, 10x Miracle White Treatment, 10x Eye Brows Growth — branded treatments create exclusivity that competitors cannot copy in their ads. “Only at 10X Healthy Skin” copy is a natural CTR driver in a crowded local market.

Max CPL guardrail = contribution margin per appointment. If you spend more acquiring a booking than you earn from it, every ad ₹ loses money. Share these figures and we set bid caps from actuals, not guesses.

13 / Safety Net
Recovery PlanThree severity levels defined before launch — fast response regardless of who notices first
Level 1 — WatchCPL rises 20–35% above target · sustained 1 week
  • Review Search Terms report — block irrelevant queries
  • Check ad copy CTR — refresh weakest performing headline
  • Verify landing page load time (<3 sec on mobile)
  • Review call conversion threshold (60 sec) — is it firing correctly?
  • Budget: no change. Normal weekly CPL variance.
Level 2 — ActCPL above max guardrail · 2 consecutive weeks
  • Pause lowest-performing service ad group
  • Shift its budget to the best-performing service campaign
  • Switch to Maximize Conversions (if not already active)
  • Tighten radius targeting to core Coimbatore city
  • Verify conversion tracking is still recording correctly
Level 3 — ResetCPL above guardrail · full calendar month
  • Full account audit: keywords, negatives, audiences, ads, landing pages
  • Rebuild campaigns with tighter match types and new RSA copy
  • Survey last 20 bookings — which keyword did they search?
  • Pause Call Ads temporarily and test form-only for 2 weeks
  • Written findings report before resuming full budget
Day-one safeguards: Daily budget cap · Bid strategy ceiling from Unit Economics · Automated alert if 7-day CPL exceeds max guardrail.
